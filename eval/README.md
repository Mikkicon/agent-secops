Kendall's Tau and Spearman's Rho - non-parametric **correlation** coefficients used to measure monotonic relationships without requiring normality. 
Kendall's Tau - concordant and discordant pairs (short, contain identical values) - mathematically more robust against ties


Spearman's Rho - the squared differences of ranked deviations (massive rank disagreement) - squares the rank differences. 
Squaring penalizes outliers heavily - ideal for correlation score to strongly reflect major individual deviations
